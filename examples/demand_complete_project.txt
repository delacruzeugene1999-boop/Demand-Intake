Demand Title: Replace legacy SWIFT messaging gateway with modernized ISO 20022 platform

Requester: Marco dela Cruz, Head of Wholesale Payments Operations
Sponsor / Accountable Owner: Anna Reyes, EVP - Transaction Banking Group
Submission Date: 2026-04-18
Business Domain: Wholesale Payments / Cash Management
Demand ID: DMD-2026-0417

Business Problem / Objective:
Our existing SWIFT MT-based messaging gateway is end-of-life with the vendor
discontinuing support in Q4 2026. We need to migrate to an ISO 20022 (MX) capable
platform that supports the BSP-mandated migration timeline, the SWIFT CBPR+ rulebook,
and our enterprise data lineage controls. The new platform must integrate with our
core banking SOR, the AML/sanctions screening engine, and the data warehouse used
by Compliance reporting.

Impacted Process / Product / Technology:
- Cross-border payments (USD, EUR, JPY corridors)
- Domestic high-value payments via PhilPaSS
- Sanctions screening (real-time)
- AML transaction monitoring (T+1)
- Regulatory reporting to BSP and AMLC

Effort Band: 200-400 person-days estimated by Architecture during preliminary scan
Cost Band: PHP 35M-45M (vendor licensing + integration + testing + cutover)
Vendor / Third Party: Shortlist of three vendors (Volante, Finastra, Bottomline);
RFP not yet issued.

Known Dependencies:
- Core banking team must expose new ISO 20022 fields via existing API gateway
- Cyber must validate vendor SOC 2 + Pen Test results
- Data team must extend the lineage catalog to cover the new gateway tables
- Regulatory Reporting team must update BSP submission schemas
